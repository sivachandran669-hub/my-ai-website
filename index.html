Onboarding sequence

Three conversations before Ledger sounds like your cofounder.

This is the actual order it happens in — each step feeds context into the next, so skipping ahead just means more back-and-forth later.

01

Tell it where the business stands

Stage, revenue, team size, the goal for the next quarter. Ledger builds a working profile instead of asking generic questions later.

02

Point it at your existing tools

Connect a doc, a spreadsheet, or just paste your notes. Ledger reads what already exists rather than making you re-explain your business from scratch.

03

Ask the question you're actually stuck on

Pricing, hiring, a slipping launch date — whatever's live right now. Every answer after this one carries the memory of everything before it.
